Build principle

A practical workflow should reveal its reasoning.

Each step is a design constraint, not a promise that software can replace analyst judgment.

01

Ingest

Read supported evidence formats and document what is not supported.

02

Normalize

Represent events and indicators in stable, testable structures.

03

Observe

Apply explicit detection logic or policy checks with visible rationale.

04

Report

Keep evidence, confidence, and limits close to the result.

05

Review

Leave the final decision to the analyst or authorized operator.
