Security headers are control surfaces.

 02Application security04 min →

Authorization is a server-side decision.

A client-side condition cannot establish access control. The service must assess actor, action, resource, and the relevant boundary.

 03Detection engineering05 min →

Authentication telemetry needs context.

Repeated failures are a review signal—not a verdict. Source, identity, method, time, and later behavior change the interpretation.
